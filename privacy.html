Ebbly Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

In short

Ebbly works only on your device. The app has no accounts, server, analytics, ads or tracking. We don't receive your data unless you send us a problem report, and we never sell it.

What is stored

  • Rules: how often the pause appears, how long it lasts, the daily limit.
  • Your choice of apps and categories. Apple hands them over as opaque tokens, so we can't see app names or time spent in them.
  • Daily counts for Insights: for each of the last 60 days, how many pauses were shown, how many times you tapped Not now, how many times you continued, whether the daily limit was reached and whether control was on that day; for Insights, in which hour of the day each pause, Not Now and Continue happened, and the pause interval that day. Days older than that are never shown or exported, and are deleted from the device the next time a pause is recorded.
  • Working state: whether the limit is reached today, whether control is on, the day you first started control, which pause thresholds already fired today, whether onboarding is done, when you acknowledged this policy, a cached Pro status, your haptics setting, whether control stopped because Pro ended, and when an app you opened for a set time gets its pause back.
  • Bedtime Mode: whether it is on, your bedtime and wake-up time, gentle or strict, the bedtime pause, and whether your Sleep Focus is on (iOS tells Ebbly only if you add Ebbly as a Focus filter).
  • Reasons you pick in the moment before opening (for example Out of Habit), with the day and the part of the day, and the app it was about as an opaque token from Apple that Ebbly cannot read.
  • Month totals: for each month, how many pauses were shown, how often you closed or continued and on how many days you stayed under the limit, kept after the daily counts are deleted.
  • Your streak: its length, your best, lifetime totals, Rest Days, the shells you earned and, for the calendar, whether each day of up to 400 days counted.
  • A log for Report a Problem: what the app and its Screen Time extensions did in the last 7 days, and crash reports iOS gives the app (see Problem reports).

All of this lives in the app's protected storage on your device (the App Group and the app's own storage) and leaves it only when you export or share it yourself.

Screen Time

The app uses system Screen Time access (Family Controls). iOS processes your app usage; we don't read it and we don't send it anywhere. You can revoke access in iOS Settings at any time.

Apple Health

With Pro, if you connect Apple Health, Ebbly reads your sleep (nothing else, and never writes) to compare your nights with the evenings before them. It is read on your iPhone each time you open the Sleep tab, never stored by Ebbly, never sent anywhere and never used for advertising. You can turn the access off in the Health app at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store. Apple processes payment details and we never receive them. Your subscription status is checked on the device with StoreKit.

Your rights

In Data & Privacy you can export all your data (JSON) and delete it. Deletion is permanent: settings, picked apps and state are erased and control stops. This is how the rights of access, portability and erasure (GDPR/UK GDPR) and the rights to know and delete under US state laws (such as CCPA/CPRA) are fulfilled. We don't sell or share personal data with third parties.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we knowingly don't collect children's data.

Problem reports

Data & Privacy has Report a Problem. It shows you the whole report before anything is sent: the app version, iOS version and iPhone model, the state of the pause chain (on or off, with no counts of apps, pauses or time), what the app and its Screen Time extensions did in the last 7 days (for example a pause shown or the shield lifted), and a one-line summary of crash reports iOS gives the app. Exactly the text shown is what is sent. It never contains app names or anything you typed. Entries older than 7 days are deleted, and the report leaves your iPhone only if you choose to email or share it. If you email it to us, we use it only to answer you and fix the problem, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Age range

Where a law requires it, Ebbly asks iOS for your age range (for example 13 to 15, or 18 and over), never your birth date. iOS shows what is shared and, where the law allows, lets you decline. Ebbly works the same for every age, so the range is not stored and never leaves your iPhone.

Changes

If the app ever handles data differently (for example, adds analytics or sync), we will update this policy and, where consent is required, ask for it before that processing starts.
