Ebbly Support

Help with Ebbly: Mindful App Limits for iPhone

Contact

Write to contact.morinapp@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days.

The pause does not appear

Ebbly needs Screen Time access. Open Ebbly: if Today says "Screen Time Is Off", tap the button there to grant access again. Then check that the apps you want are chosen and that control is on.

I changed a rule and nothing happened

After you change apps or rules while control is on, tap Apply Changes on Today. The new rules apply from then on.

An app is closed for the rest of the day

That is the daily limit. It ends at midnight. To give yourself more time today, raise the Daily Limit and tap Apply Changes.

Purchases

Ebbly Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel or change a subscription, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no subscription to cancel. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open the Ebbly Pro screen and tap Restore. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Report a problem

If Ebbly does not work as expected, open Data & Privacy and tap Report a Problem. You see the whole report first; then email it to us or share it. It shows what the app did in the last 7 days and contains no app names or anything you typed. Please add a sentence about what happened.

Your data

Everything Ebbly stores stays on your iPhone. You can export it or delete it in Data & Privacy. Read the privacy policy.
